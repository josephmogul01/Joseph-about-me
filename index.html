Introducing Joseph Mogul

Joseph Mogul is a New York City based independent journalist. He writes about political movements and histories for The Nation, Truthout, The Progressive, Mondoweiss and Jacobin.
  1. The Revolutionary Neighborliness of Minneapolis - The Progressive When ICE began terrorizing entire neighborhoods in the Twin Cities, mutual aid organizers who support unhoused people banded together to respond.
  2. A burgeoning AI boycott plasters the NYC subway - Prism Reports I embedded with some of those anti-AI artists and reported a story about a burgeoning AI boycott, resisting the pervasive narrative beamed across the subway and broadcast around the world: that AI is inevitable.
  3. Cooperative and Community at May Day Cafe KFAI Minneculture I produced a documentary-length podcast about the May Day Cafe and its connection to decades of movement history.
